Comparative Analytics Findings:

1. Event Scale Dynamics: IIT events operate on average at 1,245 attendees (over 2x the UCB average of 584) due to centralized residential campus cultures and mega-scale campus fests (OAT arenas, SAC halls).

2. Financial Models: UCB events are self-sustaining through student ticketing and corporate sponsorships ($84.1M net revenue), whereas IIT events are subsidized educational institutions focusing on maximum student footfall.

3. Turnout Reliability: Both systems demonstrate strong student turnout (74.2% for IIT vs 70.1% for UCB). In UCB events, ticketing creates a commitment mechanism that prevents no-shows.
