Slide 2: Data Cleaning Methodology
Data Cleaning (In Simple Words)

IIT Dataset Cleaning

  • Duplicate Pruning: Identified and removed 350 exact duplicate rows, reducing record count from 5,100 to 4,750.
  • Campus & Category Normalization: Fixed typos and case inconsistencies (e.g., 'iit bombay', 'IIT DELHI'); imputed missing campus tags with 'IIT Main Campus' (420 records) and categories with 'General / Academic' (445 records).
  • Missing Attendance & Budget: Replaced missing attendance (369 rows) using registration counts & median turnout (75%); filled missing budgets (484 rows) using category medians.
  • Capacity Outlier Correction: Fixed 185 unrealistic capacities (>15,000 or negative) and 461 negative attendance figures using venue size thresholds.

University (CSV) Dataset Cleaning

  • Duplicate Pruning: Removed 200 duplicate event records (subset & event ID deduplication), standardizing 10,200 rows down to 10,000.
  • Dummy Registration Purge: Replaced 1,253 placeholder entries (e.g., registrations recorded as '99999') with 85% of venue capacity.
  • Rating & Price Boundary Capping: Fixed 30 student ratings that exceeded the 5.0 ceiling (some logged as 99) and capped extreme ticket prices ($15,000 errors capped to typical $0–$100 range).
  • Financial Recalculation: Corrected 40 negative budgets; dynamically recomputed Net Revenue = (Attendance × Ticket Price + Sponsorship) − Total Costs.

Common Outlier Handling Logic

Physical Venue Law: If recorded attendance exceeded maximum hall capacity, attendance was capped to physical capacity (because 800 people cannot physically sit in a 500-seat auditorium). Original figures were preserved in audit logs for 100% transparency.

Slide 4: Dashboard Visualizations
Graphs Included In Dashboard (In Simple Words)
Monthly Footfall & Financial Trends (Line Graph)
Displays chronological surges in attendance and revenue. Shows distinct peaks during early semester orientations (August/September) and post-midterm festival seasons (January/February).
Unit / Departmental Performance (Bar & Column Charts)
Compares attendance and revenue yields across all 11 IIT campuses and 7 University faculties. Clearly illustrates which units draw the highest participation and manage budgets most efficiently.
Category Portfolio Mix (Donut & Pie Charts)
Breaks down events into Academic, Technical, Cultural, Sports, Social, and Career categories. Shows a balanced distribution (~14% to 18% each), preventing institutional over-reliance on any single activity.
3D WebGL Scatter Plot (Budget vs. Attendance vs. Satisfaction)
An interactive 3D spatial model where the X-axis is Budget, Y-axis is Attendance, and Z-axis is Satisfaction. Rotatable in 360 degrees, allowing evaluators to spot high-ROI sweet spots and isolated outliers.
3D Topographic Surface Mesh (Turnout Rate Across Units & Categories)
A continuous 3D contour terrain map visualizing turnout percentages across department/campus coordinates, identifying peak engagement zones and turnout valleys.
3D Time-Space Trajectory Curve (Temporal Evolution)
Traces how footfall and CSAT ratings evolve over time simultaneously along a continuous 3D helical path across the academic calendar.
Data Quality & Audit Health Gauges
Side-by-side indicator gauges measuring the dramatic jump in completeness, missing value mitigation (from ~10% down to 0%), and 100% capacity-compliant turnout.
